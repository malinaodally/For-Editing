Process Overview

Your Onboarding Process

Please complete the steps in order. Our team will review your submission and contact you if additional information is required.

1 Submit Information
2 Sign Agreements
3 Upload Documents
4 Training and Setup

Official onboarding statuses are updated only after Infinity Plus Financial reviews the submitted information and documents.

01 Required

Complete Your Onboarding Information

Submit your legal name, contact information, business details, PTIN, EFIN information when applicable, and required banking information.

  • Legal and contact information
  • Business information
  • PTIN information
  • EFIN information, when applicable
  • Banking and payment information
COMPLETE ONBOARDING FORM
02 Required

Review and Sign Required Agreements

Review all assigned documents carefully. Confirm that your legal name, business information, role, compensation terms, and payment information are correct before signing.

  • Tax Partner Agreement
  • Independent Contractor Agreement
  • W-9 or Business Information Form
  • Payment Authorization
  • Compliance Acknowledgment
  • Marketing and Brand Usage Agreement
REVIEW AND SIGN DOCUMENTS
03 Required

Upload Required Records Securely

Use the secure upload page to provide the records requested for your tax-partner account. Do not send sensitive documents through regular email.

  • Government-issued photo identification
  • Social Security card or EIN confirmation
  • PTIN confirmation
  • EFIN approval letter, if applicable
  • Voided check or bank verification
  • Completed W-9
  • Additional requested business records
UPLOAD DOCUMENTS SECURELY
04 Available After Review

Review Training and Setup Information

After your onboarding information and documents have been reviewed, you will receive your training schedule, software-setup details, and information about the next steps.

  • Training schedule
  • Software-access instructions
  • Policies and procedures
  • Support information
  • Final onboarding confirmation
VIEW TRAINING INFORMATION
After You Submit

What Happens After You Submit Everything?

Review

Our team checks your submitted information, signed documents, and required records.

Follow-Up

If anything is missing or needs correction, we will contact you with the next required action.

Activation

After approval, you will receive the appropriate training, system access, and onboarding confirmation.

Access is provided according to the partner’s approved role and onboarding status.

Protect Your Information

Only submit sensitive records through the secure forms and document-upload links provided on this page. Do not send Social Security numbers, banking information, identification documents, PTIN or EFIN records, or other sensitive information through regular email or text message.
